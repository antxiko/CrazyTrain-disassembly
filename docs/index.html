A sliding-tile puzzle with a train on top: the pushes, the stations, the black trains and their warnings, the crashes and the cleared stage played in GIF; the first eight boards from the cartridge's bytes, checked dot by dot against openMSX; the tunes from its own engine, and four measured bugs.

Konami for Sony - (c) Konami 1983 - 8 KB cartridge in page 0 (the dump: four copies, 32 KB) - MSX - Dump bbe8df03...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The tiles, the trains, the boards, the title and the menu come from the cartridge's bytes (checked against openMSX), and the GIFs are the cartridge running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the rules as the code has them, the tiles, the push, the speed, the crashes, the stations and the black trains, played in GIF.
  • The boards: the four boards, the shuffled ones from stage 4 and the stage cleared.
  • The sequences: the title, the menu, the demo and GAME OVER.
  • The texts: every text in the cartridge, with its translation.
  • The music: the three tunes and the chime, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: four measured bugs, one that is not, the code in RAM, the overlapping tables, the leftovers, the open questions.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, page 0 without the BIOS, the train records and their scripts, the interrupt, the RAM, the tools and the shared code.

This is documentation and preservation work: the code and artwork still belong to their authors and to Konami, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
